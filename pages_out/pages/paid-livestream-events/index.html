Paid livestream events

Sell access to a livestream without building the streaming workflow.

Castlink is for paid private livestreams where the host needs checkout, event links, viewer access, and extra-time payments without managing encoders, stream keys, or viewer accounts.

Create paid event See phone broadcast

Checkout included

Hosts choose an event package and pay through checkout before receiving the private event links.

Stream links by email

After payment, Castlink sends private broadcast and watch links. Viewer invite emails can also be sent.

Buy more time

If the event needs to run longer, the host can buy another hour and keep the same links working.

Good fit

  • One-off paid workshops
  • Private coaching sessions
  • Community and club events
  • Small venue streams

Not a social platform

Castlink does not try to grow an audience for you. It gives you the private streaming infrastructure, checkout path, and viewer links for an audience you already have or can invite directly.
