You are responsible for keeping broadcast links private and sharing watch links only with intended viewers.

3. Pricing and Paid Time

Castlink may show usage information, including broadcast time, viewer-hours, active viewers, or peak viewers. During launch, Castlink does not automatically charge surprise overage fees. If more time is needed, the host can purchase an optional extension before the event expires.

Free test streams do not include paid event rights, paid support expectations, or extension purchases. They are for setup checks only and may have lower viewer limits or shorter unused-link expiry than paid events.

4. Extensions

Extensions add time to the same event and keep the same broadcast and watch links. Extension purchases are processed separately through Stripe. Extensions should be purchased before the stream expires.

Viewer capacity upgrades may be offered for live paid events. Capacity upgrades increase the concurrent viewer limit for the same event and keep the same watch link. Castlink does not automatically charge viewer overage fees during launch.
