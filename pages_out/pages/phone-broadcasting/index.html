Direct phone broadcast

Go live from your phone browser. No broadcast software required.

Open the private broadcast link on your phone, allow camera and microphone, check the preview, and go live. Viewers use a separate private watch link, so they never see host controls.

No OBS required

The main path is direct browser broadcasting from a phone or laptop. No encoder setup, stream key, or broadcast software is needed.

Camera preview first

The broadcast page previews the camera before going live, with camera and microphone selection available from the controls.

Private viewer link

Viewers do not need the broadcast controls. They use the separate watch link sent after event creation.

Good for first-time hosts

The host does not need streaming software, encoder settings, stream keys, or platform experience. The workflow is built around a simple phone camera broadcast.

When to use it

  • Quick private events
  • Small venue streams
  • Workshops and classes
  • Community updates
  • Simple paid broadcasts
