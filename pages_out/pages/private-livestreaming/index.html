Private livestreaming

Private livestreams without sending viewers to social media.

Castlink gives the host a private broadcast link and viewers a separate watch link. It is built for controlled events where the audience should see the stream, not comments, ads, accounts, or a public feed.

Create private stream Broadcast from phone

No viewer accounts

Viewers open the watch link. That keeps access simple for schools, clubs, venues, workshops, and private groups.

No comments or algorithm

The stream is the product. There is no public feed, no ad placement, and no social platform distraction around the event.

Private host controls

The broadcast link is separate from the watch link, so host access stays private.

Who it is for

  • Community groups and clubs
  • Schools and training sessions
  • Small venues and ticketed events
  • Coaches, workshops, and private creators

How it works

  • Create the event and pay once.
  • Open the private broadcast link on the host device.
  • Share the private watch link with viewers.
  • Extend the event if you need more time.
