For local organisations

Livestream events for clubs, schools, venues and community groups.

Castlink keeps livestreaming practical for small organisations: create an event, open the broadcast link on a phone, and share the watch link with people who cannot attend in person.

Simple setup

  • No broadcast software required.
  • No viewer accounts required.
  • No public social feed.
  • Phone browser broadcasting available.

Operational control

The host keeps the broadcast link private and shares the viewer link only with the intended audience. Admin tools help track events, reports, expiry, and usage.
